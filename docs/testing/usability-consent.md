## What this is

We're students on the Intelligent and Inclusive Classroom project at Ashesi. We're building a browser tool that changes how course pages look, for example by adding more space between lines, hiding clutter or showing reading progress, so that pages are easier to read.

We'd like to watch you try the tool on some practice course pages and hear what you think. **We're testing the tool, not you.** If something is confusing, that's a problem with the tool, and it's useful for us to know.

## What you'll do

- About **10 minutes**, on our laptop, in a fresh browser profile. You won't need to log in to anything.
- Five short tasks on practice pages we made up, for example "make this page easier to focus on". We'll ask you to say what you're thinking as you go. Each task has a time limit to keep the session short. Running out of time is fine and tells us something useful.
- After each task, one quick question about how easy it was. At the end, two rating questions and two questions about your opinions.

## What we'll record

- **Written notes only.** No audio, video, screen recording or photos.
- What you did, how long it took, what you said, and your answers to our questions.
- **We will not ask about any health condition, disability or diagnosis.** You don't need to tell us anything like that, and if you mention it we won't write it down.

## How we protect your information

- Your notes are kept under a code (like "U03"), not your name.
- The list linking your name to your code is only for arranging the session. We delete it once all sessions are done.
- Raw notes stay in a private team folder. They never go on GitHub, in issues or in group chats.
- Our public report includes only **combined results** (for example "4 of 5 people found the button"). We won't use quotes that could identify you.
- We delete the raw notes when the project is graded.

## Your choices

- Taking part is **completely voluntary**.
- You can **skip any task or question**, or **stop at any time**, without giving a reason. Nothing bad happens if you do, and it has no effect on your grades or your relationship with anyone on the team.
- Until our report is published (around 10 December 2026), you can ask us to remove your notes. Contact the session moderator: *[moderator's preferred contact, filled in before sessions]*.

## Questions

You can ask us anything before, during or after the session.
